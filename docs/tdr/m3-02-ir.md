# TDR-M3-2 — IR shape (expr nodes, fuel unit)

## Context

Fuel accounting needs a countable unit, and lowering needs a stable
target smaller than the full AST. Without a fixed IR node definition,
"1 unit per node" is unenforceable.

## Decision

IR is expression-node based: each expression lowers to IR expr nodes,
and each node costs exactly 1 fuel unit charged once on first
evaluation. Statements and blocks structure evaluation but do not add
separate fuel charges beyond their expressions.

## Consequences

- Fuel costs are predictable and auditable from the IR.
- IR stays minimal; new syntax must define its node expansion and
  hence its fuel cost up front.
- Optimizations must preserve node-charge correspondence or redefine
  the rule.
